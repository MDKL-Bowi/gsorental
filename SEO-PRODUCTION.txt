GSO RENTAL - PRODUCTION SEO CHECKLIST

Preview deployments intentionally use:
<meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow">

When gsorental.com is ready:
1. Change robots meta on every HTML page to index,follow.
2. Add a canonical URL to every page using its production URL.
3. Add og:url and a 1200x630 og:image / twitter:image.
4. Keep robots.txt and sitemap.xml at the site root.
5. Add each real article URL to sitemap.xml when published.
6. Connect gsorental.com to Google Search Console after DNS is active.
7. Replace the map placeholder with the confirmed business / pickup location only after the client approves it.
8. Replace placeholder social links / email notes with verified production details.
