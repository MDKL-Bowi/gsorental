When gsorental.com goes live:
1. Change <meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow"> to <meta name="robots" content="index,follow">.
2. Add: <link rel="canonical" href="https://gsorental.com/">
3. Add: <meta property="og:url" content="https://gsorental.com/">
4. Add a 1200x630 social sharing image and set og:image / twitter:image.
5. Deploy robots.txt and sitemap.xml from the templates in this project.
6. Register the domain with Google Search Console after DNS is active.
